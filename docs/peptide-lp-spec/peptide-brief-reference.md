## Individual Peptides (with internal clinical protocols)

### Healing & Recovery

**BPC-157**
- Mechanism: Derived from gastric juice protein. Drives angiogenesis into damaged tissue, modulates local inflammation via nitric oxide. Works on gut lining, tendons, ligaments, nerves, blood vessels. Oral stable in stomach acid.
- Clinical protocols:
  - UC, IBD, gut repair: 500 mcg oral 1–2x daily
  - Tendon, ligament, wound healing: 250–500 mcg SC daily
  - Nerve regeneration: 250–500 mcg SC daily
  - High-load systemic: up to 1000 mcg SC daily
- Notes: WADA/USADA prohibited — screen athletes. Oral for GI targets; SC for everything else.

**TB-500**
- Mechanism: Synthetic fragment of Thymosin Beta-4. Controls cell migration via actin regulation. Pairs with BPC-157: BPC builds blood supply, TB-500 gets cells there. Longer half-life — weekly maintenance.
- Clinical protocols:
  - Acute injury/wound (loading): 2–4 mg SC 2x/week × 4–6 weeks
  - Maintenance/chronic repair: 2 mg SC weekly
- Notes: WADA/USADA prohibited.

**KPV**
- Mechanism: Three-amino-acid fragment from alpha-MSH. Enters cells via PepT1 transporter (upregulated in IBD). Shuts down NF-kB. Stabilizes mast cells, reduces histamine-driven symptoms. Works oral, SC, topical.
- Clinical protocols:
  - IBD, gut inflammation: 500 mcg oral 1–2x daily
  - Systemic inflammation, skin conditions: 200–500 mcg SC daily
  - Wound healing, atopic dermatitis: 0.05–0.1% topical 1–2x daily
- Notes: Pairs with BPC-157 for IBD (BPC rebuilds lining, KPV turns off fire).

### Metabolic & Longevity

**MOTS-c**
- Mechanism: Mitochondria-derived during metabolic stress. Activates AMPK → more mitochondria, faster glucose pull from blood. Animal data on bone density via osteoblasts. Works best in patients already training but not getting metabolic response.
- Clinical protocols:
  - Obesity, insulin resistance: 5–10 mg SC 3x/week
  - Bone density: 5–10 mg SC 3x/week
  - Metabolic optimization: 5 mg SC 2–3x/week
- Notes: Supplement folate and B12. Track fasting glucose, HbA1c, insulin at baseline and 12 weeks. DEXA every 6 months for bone work.

**Epitalon**
- Mechanism: Four-amino-acid peptide modeled on pineal gland extract. Activates telomerase, lengthens telomeres in human cells. Restores melatonin production — sleep improvement is the first thing patients notice. Russian longevity data. Dosing is cyclic (short bursts 2x/year, not daily).
- Clinical protocols:
  - Longevity, telomere maintenance: 5–10 mg SC daily × 10–20 days, 2–3x/year
  - Insomnia, circadian disruption: 5–10 mg SC daily × 10–20 days, 2x/year
- Notes: Evening dosing. Best responders have disrupted sleep architecture (not just trouble falling asleep).

### Neuro & Cognitive

**Semax**
- Mechanism: Synthetic ACTH fragment. Increases BDNF, NGF, serotonin in brain. Clinical: better focus, working memory, attention. Used in Russia for decades for stroke/TBI.
- Clinical protocols:
  - Cognitive enhancement, focus: 750–1500 mcg intranasal daily
  - TBI, post-concussion, cerebral ischemia: 1500–3000 mcg intranasal daily
  - Migraine, trigeminal neuralgia: 1000–2000 mcg intranasal during episode + 3–5 days
- Notes: Morning or early afternoon — evening dosing wrecks sleep. Cycle 5 on / 2 off for chronic use.

**Emideltide (DSIP)**
- Mechanism: Nine-amino-acid neuropeptide. Normalizes sleep architecture (not sedating). Dials down CRH, promotes delta-wave deep sleep. Interacts with GABA and opioid systems without dependence. 97% symptom resolution in opioid withdrawal, 87% in alcohol withdrawal. No next-day grogginess.
- Clinical protocols:
  - Chronic insomnia: 100–300 mcg SC nightly
  - Opioid withdrawal: 200–500 mcg SC daily × 5–10 days
  - Narcolepsy: 100–200 mcg SC nightly
  - Chronic pain (adjunct): 100–300 mcg SC nightly
- Notes: 30–60 minutes before bed. Withdrawal — adjunct only, not monotherapy.
