## Accuracy Rules

- Wolverine Stack = BPC-157 + TB-500.
- Structural Repair Stack at Eden = BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV from the Eden peptide brief.
- Warrior Stack is not named in the Eden peptide brief and public usage appears inconsistent. Do not publish BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV as "Warrior Stack" unless the provider/vendor confirms that exact product naming.
- KLOW Stack = BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV + GHK-Cu.
- GLOW Stack = BPC-157 + TB-500 + GHK-Cu.
- Do not call Warrior Stack "KLOW" unless GHK-Cu is added.
- Do not call BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV "KLOW" unless GHK-Cu is added.
- Do not call BPC-157 + TB-500 "GLOW" unless GHK-Cu is added.
- MOTS-c, Epitalon, Semax, KPV, BPC-157, and TB-500 are usually sold by compound name rather than a universal stack name.
- Emideltide / DSIP can use "Delta Sleep" as the consumer-facing stack name if the provider approves that positioning.

## Compliance Guardrails

Do not publish peptide dosing, route, frequency, or protocol instructions on public landing pages. Keep public pages focused on goals, eligibility, consult-first medical screening, and provider-approved benefits language.

Use:

- "May support"
- "Designed for"
- "Clinician-guided"
- "Personalized after consultation"
- "Eligibility and protocol are determined by the provider"

Avoid:

- Disease cure claims
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Public dosage instructions
- Route/frequency tables
- Athlete-safe claims
- WADA/USADA-safe claims
- Positioning prescription or compounded peptides as casual supplements
